Changes from Nyx's drafts made in review:

- **FG-44:** added the "read the response directly" correct form and a cross-reference to FG-33/FG-34.
- **FG-46:** flagged as standard Jinja2 scoping rather than an HA addition. Kept because HA templates are where users hit it.
- **FG-48:** `context.user_id` is `None`, not `''`. Severity lowered to soft_fail: either policy for internal callers is acceptable, and what is scored is having an explicit rule for `None`.
- **FG-49:** the correct form now distinguishes existence (`states[x] is not none`) from availability (`has_value()`).
- **FG-51:** added `delay_off` on a template binary sensor as the first correct form, and completed the trigger-based form with a `state:`.
- **FG-52:** the `for:` correct form had no from/to filter, which broke FG-43. Fixed.
- **FG-53:** reframed around the HA-native fix (send only the change; a trigger-based template entity merges it) instead of a ZenOS-style keyed store.
- **FG-54:** added `script.turn_on` to a worker script as a correct form.
- **FG-55:** deprecation start corrected to 2026.10 from the release notes. `end_type: "removal"` was not a valid value; the end stays null until the removal version is confirmed.

### From the HA release notes

| FG | Title | Severity | HA |
|---|---|---|---|
| FG-57 | Sun and calendar trigger offset without offset_type changed direction | hard_fail | 2026.11 |
| FG-58 | Matching on friendly_name instead of entity_id or the name functions | soft_fail | 2026.11 |
| FG-59 | Removed or renamed purpose-specific trigger and condition keys | hard_fail | 2026.5–2026.7 |

- **FG-57:** HA 2026.11 (PR #184855): `sun.*`, `calendar.event_started` and `calendar.event_ended` triggers used to default to `offset_type: before`. Now the sign decides. YAML written with an offset and no `offset_type` silently fires on the other side of the event.
- **FG-58:** the 2026.11 naming change and child devices change some `friendly_name` values. Entity IDs do not change. HA recommends `entity_name()`, `device_name()` and `area_name()` and plans to deprecate `friendly_name`.
- **FG-59:** `entered_home`/`left_home`/`is_home`/`is_not_home` removed in 2026.5; Labs `any`/`last` renamed in 2026.6; ten purpose-specific keys renamed in 2026.7. The old keys stop working.

## Open items before ratification

- FG-35: confirm the HA version where reload became changed-only; re-check the `reload_all` and `script.reload` notes.
- FG-41: confirm the HA version that added `as_datetime(x, default)`, and whether `as_datetime` accepts a bare date. Nyx's `as_local` note (naive treated as UTC) was not merged; HA may treat naive datetimes as local.

- FG-53: burst-test that a trigger-based template entity processes back-to-back events without interleaving.
- FG-55: confirm the 2027.10 removal version.

- FG-58: confirm the name-function signatures.
- Pending candidate: HA 2026.10 made state conditions that combine `for:` with an attribute, a list of states or another entity's state fail validation (2026.10.2 re-allowed lists). Source was a page summary; verify before drafting.
- FG-48: the admin-required action lists (2026.9, 2026.10) came from page summaries; verify.
